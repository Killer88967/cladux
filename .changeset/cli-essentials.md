New commands: `list`, `search`, `view`, `docs`, `info`, `diff` and `update`. `update` brings components up to date and keeps the files you changed, using fingerprints the CLI now saves in `cl-lock.json`. `add` gets `--dry-run`, `--view`, `--diff` and `--path`, and every command takes `--silent`. `list` marks new components, and `-v` prints the version.
